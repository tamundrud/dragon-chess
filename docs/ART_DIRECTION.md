# Art Direction

## Overall style
Use an illustrated children's storybook-fantasy look:
- Hand-drawn energy.
- Inked outlines.
- Light painted texture.
- Expressive silhouettes.
- Uneven, lively shapes.
- Humour and personality.
- Warm fantasy atmosphere.
- Strong readability.

Avoid:
- Photorealism.
- Generic glossy mobile-game style.
- Movie-like 3D designs.
- Emoji or tiny icon pieces.
- Flat corporate vector art.
- Crude programmer art presented as final.
- Gore or realistic injury.

## Board environment
The board should resemble a carved Viking game board on a warm wooden or map-covered surface.

## Board tokens
Tokens must:
- Be identifiable at phone size.
- Have distinct silhouettes.
- Show faction and piece type.
- Read against light and dark squares.
- Not rely only on labels.
- Avoid detail that disappears at small scale.

Temporary tokens should be deliberate silhouettes or portraits, not fake final art.
