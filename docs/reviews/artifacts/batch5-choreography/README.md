# Batch 5 choreography — approved staging proposal

[View the six-pose storyboard](storyboard.png). The owner approved these choreography directions on November 4, 2026. This is a schematic staging proposal, not production artwork, a recording, or evidence of a working implementation. Attacker and weapon are drawn in front of the grey defender; the gold star denotes intended contact. The existing board remains visible behind the combatants as in the approved Fireworm benchmark.

- **Valhallarama:** establish a visible gap; draw back and aim; take a short grounded approach; plant on the original side of the defender; extend the sword for a deliberate, readable playful tap; withdraw and recover. No vault, crossing behind, or blade extension during descent. A suitable right-facing contact pose must be made or refined: the existing contact image points left, so the sketch does not imply the present asset can produce this pose unchanged. Place contact at an appropriate visible upper-body or head anchor for each defender rather than at a fixed screen coordinate.
- **Mogadon:** establish separation, brace behind the shield, charge only to shield range, make visible shield-to-defender contact, overbalance away, and regain his stance. Keep the shield and contact in the foreground.
- **Hairy Scary Librarian:** begin at a distance in crossed guard, approach without crossing the defender, show the first blade reaching the defender, then show a readable two-blade contact before withdrawing. Keep both connected to his hands and individually distinguishable over the defender.

The drawings specify spatial relationships and beat order, not final dimensions, duration, effects, expressions, or finished anatomy. Check actual transparent bounds, facing and anchors at phone scale for every pose and relevant defender. Pose changes should preserve connected silhouettes and continuity, and impact should coincide with visible contact and reaction. The foreground ordering must hold throughout overlap, including transitions and recovery; effects must not hide the weapon or shield.
