## Record and decision rules

Use a review build made from PR #10's recorded full head SHA; do not test an older hosted build. Building/serving an acceptance build is a later operator action, not a deployment performed by this reassessment. Record tester/date, full SHA, device/model, OS/browser version, viewport/orientation, display scale, settings, steps, observed result and photo/video/measurement link for each gate. Repeat on physical iOS Safari and Android Chrome, plus desktop mouse and tablet where applicable. Include approximately 360px and 390px portrait, short landscape, high-density screens, and expanded/collapsed browser bars.

- **PASS:** all listed criteria hold on every required device/settings combination, with recorded evidence.
- **FAIL:** any listed failure occurs. Record the smallest reproduction; a reproducible crash, stuck/blank board, wrong/double move, or unreachable required control blocks acceptance and warrants a separately scoped blocking-defect fix.
- **BLOCKED:** required device, assets, tester, evidence, setup or agreed budget is unavailable. This never counts as PASS.
- **DEFERRED:** only a named product/merge owner can explicitly accept a specified risk for a specified milestone, with reason and follow-up. No deferrals are granted here. A failed gameplay/lifecycle gate must be resolved before acceptance.

All rows currently have result **NOT RUN**, owner **unassigned**, and evidence **none**. Copy one result record per device/combination; a single successful screenshot cannot close a row.

## Repeatable setups

Start a new local pass-and-play game for each recipe. Coordinates are source then destination, alternating sides; let earlier captures finish unless the step asks for Skip. These recipes were checked for legality with `chess.js`, not played on a physical device.

| Recipe | Moves from the initial board | Expected checkpoint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| G: generic capture | e2-e4, d7-d5, e4xd5 | White pawn on d5, black pawn removed, Black to move; Undo restores white pawn e4 and black pawn d5, White to move |
| F: Fire Dragon | g1-f3, e7-e5, f3xe5 | White knight on e5, black pawn removed, Black to move; Undo restores knight f3 and black pawn e5, White to move |
| P: quiet promotion | a2-a4, b8-c6, a4-a5, h7-h5, a5-a6, h5-h4, a6xb7, h4-h3, b7-b8 | Promotion choice before commitment; chosen white piece on b8, Black to move |
| C: capturing promotion | a2-a4, h7-h5, a4-a5, h5-h4, a5-a6, h4-h3, a6xb7, h3xg2, b7xa8 | Promotion choice; chosen white piece replaces captured black rook a8, Black to move |
| M: checkmate | f2-f3, e7-e5, g2-g4, d8-h4 | Checkmate; Black winner displayed; no further board move accepted |
| D: repetition draw | g1-f3, g8-f6, f3-g1, f6-g8; repeat the same four moves | Draw displayed after the second cycle |

## Checklist

| Gate | Procedure | PASS criteria | FAIL criteria |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Physical layout and input | On each physical phone, open menu/game/settings; expand/collapse browser bars, rotate both ways and scroll short landscape. Select/deselect a piece; play e2-e4 and attempt illegal e7-e4. Repeat with a thumb and desktop mouse. | Board squares and piece identities are readable without zoom; status and required controls remain reachable, clear of notches/browser bars; no horizontal page scroll; selection corresponds to touched square; illegal move changes neither board nor turn. Vertical scrolling reaches controls without making unintended moves. | Clipped/covered control, unreadable board/status, wrong-square tap, accidental move while scrolling, horizontal overflow, illegal move or persistent blank canvas. |
| 2. Exact capture and Skip | Run G and F separately. Let each finish naturally; repeat with one deliberate Skip early, during strike/impact and late recovery. During the attack try board taps and Undo/Restart. After completion Undo once, recapture, then make a legal Black move. | One attack and one captured piece; attack input locked, Undo/Restart disabled; Skip reachable and completes once; final board/turn matches recipe; one Undo restores exactly the pre-capture position; next move works, with no stale effects or extra move. | Capture tap immediately skips unintentionally; duplicate capture/turn; locked board after completion; stale effects; wrong Undo; Skip unreachable or ineffective. |
| 3. Rotation during active capture | Repeat G and F at Normal speed, rotate during preparation, strike, impact and recovery, rotate back, then finish naturally and repeat with Skip. Include browser-bar changes. | Attacker, defender and relevant effects remain visibly composed inside the canvas; labels and Skip readable/reachable; scene resumes and commits once; final board correct, one canvas, no crash/blank/stuck scene. | Clipping hides combatants/labels/Skip, persistent blank render, restarted/double timeline, wrong board or lost input. A blank attachment without direct observation is inconclusive: record and retest with screen video. |
| 4. Leave/re-enter and interruption | Leave G/F mid-capture through Menu, start New Game and play e2-e4; repeat five times. Background/foreground or lock/unlock the phone during a capture and after return to menu. | New game always has initial pieces/White turn; e2-e4 works once; no old attack, late move or duplicate canvas appears. Foreground resumes or completes safely with a correct playable board. | Old-game callbacks change a new game, duplicate canvas, crash, lost input or indefinitely blank scene. |
| 5. Settings and motion invariant | For G/F compare Normal, Fast, Instant, animations disabled, and reduced motion. Close/reopen Settings and reload to verify preferences. Compare resulting board/turn and Undo. | Every setting produces the same recipe checkpoint and one-move Undo. Preferences persist; reduced motion is comfortable with no distressing movement/flash. Tester explicitly approves motion readability at Normal/Fast and clear state transitions at Instant/off. | Different chess result, locked controls, settings lost, effects persist, or motion/flash causes discomfort or makes the capture unreadable. |
| 6. Promotion and phone overlays | On both phone orientations run P and C. At choice, Cancel first: verify board/turn unchanged, then retry. On separate fresh games choose Queen/Rook/Bishop/Knight; complete and Undo before game-over. | All four choices and Cancel readable/reachable; no move committed on Cancel; chosen piece and single turn update correct; capturing promotion removes only the target rook and finishes its presentation; Undo restores the pawn and captured piece. | Obscured option, wrong piece/turn, commitment on Cancel, duplicate/blocked promotion or wrong restored board. |
| 7. Check/status/end of game | Run M and D. For check before mate use a cooperative legal game. Inspect status in narrow portrait and short landscape; Restart after game-over. | Check, Black checkmate win and draw labels fully readable; board rejects post-game moves; Restart returns initial board/White turn and allows e2-e4. Game-over Undo is currently disabled; do not expect it to be available. | Truncated/covered or wrong result, board permits post-game play, Restart fails, status obscures other controls. Stalemate/other special draws need a separately recorded legal game; M/D do not prove all draws. |
| 8. Intended child | With an adult observing but not coaching, ask the intended child to start a game, move, capture, Skip, Undo, Restart, change settings, select/cancel promotion using prepared P/C positions, and return to menu. | Child completes each task unaided and can identify side to move, captured piece and chosen promotion; observer records confusion/comfort feedback. | Assistance is needed to find/operate a required control or interpret game state; distress or repeated accidental actions. If child testing is unavailable, BLOCKED. |
| 9. Art and composition | Review G/F at phone sizes and tablet/desktop; inspect primary combatants, effect edges, text, contrast and fitted-art size; watch complete captures. | Product owner explicitly accepts temporary lettered/procedural art for this milestone and both captures remain readable; Fire Dragon action meets the vertical-slice quality expectation. | Art or fitted size prevents understanding; owner rejects current quality. Missing illustrated assets/other bespoke presenters may only be DEFERRED explicitly, never described as shipped. |
| 10. Audio | First establish approved real sound assets in a separately authorized task/build. On hardware test first interaction unlock, mute/unmute, volume endpoints, background/interruption and return. Also test absent/rejected audio. | Approved cues audible only when intended, mute silent, volume behaves correctly, no duplicate/late cue; missing/rejected sound never blocks a move or capture. | Unexpected sound while muted, unresponsive game, duplicate/late audio, or failed agreed playback. Current build ships no sound pack: audible acceptance is BLOCKED; silence is not proof of success. |
| 11. Performance and stability | Agree device-specific budgets before execution. Record build/device, baseline after five warm-up cycles, then 20 G/F capture/Skip/menu cycles, frame timing, touch response, memory at comparable settled points and any thermal throttling. | Recorded results meet an explicitly approved frame-time/input/memory budget; no crash, persistent stall, accumulating canvases or reproducible upward retained-resource trend. | Any budget breach, crash/stall or accumulating live scenes/canvases. Without an approved quantitative budget and measurements, BLOCKED; no numeric performance acceptance is granted by this document. |
