# Four Bishop Battle Scenes - Plan

## Goal Capsule
Deliver four distinct illustrated Bishop captures for owner visual acceptance while preserving chess and the Fireworm benchmark.

## Product Contract
### Requirements
- R1. Give Hiccup, small Toothless, Thuggory and silver Killer original, expressive fantasy artwork and distinct attacks, following the roster and art direction.
- R2. Preserve identity through moves, Undo and deterministic same-faction Bishop promotion; retain the existing chess lifecycle, Skip, cancellation, settings and responsive staging.
- R3. Cover legal capture and lifecycle behavior automatically and provide portrait and landscape recordings for each character.
- R4. Record the prior knight batch's owner approval and deliver a reviewed draft PR against main, stopping for Bishop visual acceptance.

## Planning Contract
Follow `docs/FIREWORM_BENCHMARK.md` and the knight presenter for connected foreground staging, identity, effects and cleanup. Keep attack choreography individual. Keep assets economical and avoid unnecessary startup downloads. No rules changes, deployment, merge or further characters.

## Implementation Units
1. Add and verify starting and promotion identities; produce original art with provenance and practical loading.
2. Implement four individual scenes using shared lifecycle and defender staging; verify completion, Skip, cancellation, settings and rotation.
3. Exercise legal captures on desktop and mobile, record all eight views, review the change and address findings, run repository checks and open a draft PR.

## Verification Contract
Run unit tests, typecheck, lint, build, production preview and relevant Playwright coverage on desktop and both phone orientations. Inspect recordings for staging and motion; automated results do not substitute for owner visual acceptance or physical-device testing.

## Definition of Done
All four scenes work through normal play, verification and review are complete, recordings are directly linked in the draft PR, and the owner can judge each Bishop. No merge or deployment.
