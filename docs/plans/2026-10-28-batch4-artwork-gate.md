# Batch 4 pre-implementation artwork gate

## Goal and boundary
Prepare and inspect original storybook artwork for Stoick's approved physical-comedy choreography and a visually consistent Flamehuffer fire pose. Deliver visual evidence for owner review. Do not implement scenes, open a PR, merge, or deploy before explicit artwork approval.

## Work and evidence

1. Revise complete Stoick poses for charge, slip, tumble, confused landing and proud recovery, retaining a connected, legible axe. Explore an accidental shield contact without injury.
2. Revise Flamehuffer from the existing defender artwork, preserving its silhouette, blue-grey and turquoise palette, gold collar and scarf. Inspect the transparent edges and whole silhouette.
3. Inspect all revised cutouts on light and dark backgrounds at desktop and phone-emulated portrait and landscape sizes. Record source provenance, remaining ambiguities, and links to stills. Static poses do not establish continuous rotation, contact timing, animation performance or physical-device acceptance.
4. Stop for the owner's visual decision. Subsequent implementation requires that decision and its own technical verification and review.
