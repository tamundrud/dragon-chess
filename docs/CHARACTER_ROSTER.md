# Character roster — faction casting and design brief

**Casting status (2026-10-25):** Factions and named King/Queen/Bishop roles are **chosen**. **Eight different book-named dragon candidates** are now proposed for rooks/knights—four different names per tribe, rather than duplicates. Their assignments, precise fire capability, artwork and animations are **not yet owner-approved**. This is a design brief; the current playable build still uses prototype registries, lettered tokens and mostly generic presenters.

## Chess factions

- **White: Hairy Hooligans**, from Berk. A warmer, more welcoming visual family; distinguish with banners, warm light, distinctive Viking accents and token frames. Palette values are candidates, not locked.
- **Black: Meathead clan / Merciless Meatheads**, a rival Viking tribe. Contrasting darker/cooler or earthier tones, shapes, banners and markings; avoid equating the tribe with inherent evil.
- **Both sides:** eight barbarian Viking warrior pawns; primarily dragon-based rooks and knights; clear light/dark allegiance on every piece. No duplicated dragon *names* across tribes.
- The user selects the named *book* characters, while art should be an original richly coloured 2D animated-storybook interpretation, not designs from film/television adaptations.

## Book-canon and character notes

- **Stoick / Valhallarama:** commanding Viking leaders, with different posture and personality, not interchangeable crowns.
- **Mogadon:** named chief of the Meatheads, rival of Stoick.
- **Hairy Scary Librarian:** a feared, formidable **male** Meathead librarian. A chess “queen” is the most mobile game piece; its actor need not be female.
- **Hiccup:** the smaller, clever young Viking of the *books*, not his screen-adaptation model.
- **Toothless:** a **small book dragon**; not the movie Night Fury. Distinctive silhouette and expressive character matter more than matching a large-dragon power fantasy. His inclusion as a bishop does **not** imply fire-breathing rook abilities.
- **Thuggory:** son of Mogadon and young Meathead warrior; spell the name **Thuggory**. He is paired here with his dragon **Killer**, a huge silver **Monstrous Nightmare** in the books.
- **Dragon rooks:** Wodensfang and Green Death (White); Furious and Luna (Black) are proposed as the fire-breathing showpieces. Green Death's green fire is specifically attested; sea-dragon species generally have fire abilities, but particular fire-breathing acts were not individually established in this research for every proposed named rook. If book-source verification changes the appropriateness of a character, switch to a book fire-breathing species (e.g. Flamehuffer) *without inventing a named character*. Do not assert an undocumented individual feat as canon.
- **Dragon knights:** White Windwalker (Hiccup's fast riding dragon) and Stormfly (Camicazi's colour-changing Mood-Dragon); Black One Eye (Sabre-Tooth Driver Dragon) and Typhoon (Rocket Ripper). These are confirmed book-world names, **not** film versions. In particular, book Stormfly is **not** a Deadly Nadder.
- **Excluded:** Toothless and Killer already hold bishop positions; Fireworm, Hookfang and Ripperclaw are Monstrous Nightmares and must not be introduced as rooks/knights. Avoid screen-only dragon names.
- **Canon vs gameplay casting:** Green Death is antagonistic to Hiccup; Luna is allied with Furious. Their opposite-side chess assignment is deliberate non-canon tournament casting, not a claim about original book tribal loyalty.
- **Two-tribe expression:** silhouettes must signal *piece type* first and *faction* second, with tribe-coded colour trims, armour/saddles, banners or frames, and accessible contrast on both square colours.
- **Fictional casting vs chess semantics:** humans can occupy queen/bishop roles regardless of gender or fighting style, and role casting does not change legal chess movement.
