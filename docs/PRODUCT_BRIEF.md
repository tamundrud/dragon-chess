# Product Brief

## Working title
Dragon Chess

The name is temporary and should not be treated as a public commercial title.

## Concept
Create a child-friendly chess game that follows normal chess rules but makes captures dramatic and playful. A capture briefly transitions to a character-specific action scene before returning to the board.

The game is for a child who already likes chess and dragons. The goal is to make chess more imaginative without altering the rules.

## Core promise
> Every capture feels like a short character moment, while the chess game remains clear, correct, and easy to play.

## Primary user
- Approximately eight years old.
- Already understands chess.
- Enjoys dragons, fantasy, humour, and heroic characters.
- Plays on a phone or computer.

## Initial experience
The first usable version supports:
- Local pass-and-play.
- Touch and mouse controls.
- Mobile portrait and landscape.
- Desktop.
- Legal-move highlighting.
- Check, checkmate, stalemate, and draws.
- Castling, en passant, and promotion.
- Undo and restart.
- Attack animations.
- Normal, Fast, and Instant animation modes.
- Skip control.
- Reduced-motion mode.
- Local preference storage.

## Vertical-slice target
The first vertical slice proves:
1. Chess rules are reliable.
2. The board is readable on a phone.
3. Captures enter and leave an attack scene without corrupting state.
4. One fire-dragon capture looks meaningfully better than ordinary web UI animation.

Other characters may use deliberate temporary silhouettes.

## Creative source and quality bar

- The characters, setting, humour and visual references come from **Cressida Cowell's *How to Train Your Dragon* books**, **not** DreamWorks films or television.
- This is a private family game with no planned public release or commercial use. Revisit asset rights before any distribution.
- The chess game should remain legally correct and clear; the book-themed presentation should make the experience memorable rather than change chess mechanics.
- Fireworm’s illustrated rook capture is accepted after owner physical-phone testing as the [battle-scene quality benchmark](FIREWORM_BENCHMARK.md). Board tokens and most character art remain placeholders; the Fire Dragon is procedural and no sound pack ships.
- Extend the coherent book-first visual treatment only through separately scoped character work, preserving character-specific attacks and the approved quality standard.
- See [the visual reset plan](plans/2026-10-25-book-visual-reset.md) for approval gates before another animation implementation phase.

## Approved family-game factions and roles (2026-10-25)

- White plays as the **Hairy Hooligans** from Berk; Black as their rivals, the **Meathead clan**.
- White king **Stoick the Vast**, queen **Valhallarama**, bishops **Hiccup** and **book Toothless**.
- Black king **Mogadon the Meathead**, queen **Hairy Scary Librarian**, bishops **Thuggory** and his dragon **Killer**.
- Both sides have **barbarian warrior pawns**; all rook/knight castings are **approved** and differ across factions. White rooks **Fireworm / Hookfang**, knights **Windwalker / One Eye**; Black rooks **unnamed Flamehuffer / Common or Garden Dragon**, knights **unnamed Driller-Dragon / Poisonous Piffleworm**. Both rooks per side breathe fire. The two Meathead rook species are plausible faction portrayals, **not attested individually owned Meathead dragons**.
- **Promotion rule approved:** normal chess choice of Queen, Rook, Bishop or Knight; the promoted token reuses an approved character design of that **same faction and piece class**. Duplicate appearances are allowed; no additional characters or new promotion moves.
- The richly painted storybook fantasy **environment/UI style** is preferred and the **entire piece roster is approved**, but individual character artwork, full board depiction and animation performance still require visual approval. [Roster](CHARACTER_ROSTER.md) is authoritative for casting and status.
