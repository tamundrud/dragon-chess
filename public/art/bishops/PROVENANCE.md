# Bishop battle artwork provenance

These original candidate illustrations were generated with OpenAI image generation for this project. No book illustrations, film imagery, or third-party character art were used as references. The starting point was an original fantasy/Viking character sheet generated for the batch, plus an independently generated small green dragon. The ready poses were isolated with image-tool edits; action and recovery poses were edited from those ready poses. Thuggory's recovery was revised to keep the shield on the same side; Killer's recovery was recomposed to keep all feet in frame. The three complete poses per character, plus Hiccup’s release pose and glider, are individually exported, not cut from an overlapping sheet.

Hiccup is an inventive young Viking with a wooden glider; Toothless is a small green, toothless dragon; Thuggory is a shield-bearing rival with blue-grey fur; Killer is a silver winged dragon. These are independent interpretations of the approved roles, not official franchise designs. Action effects are animated separately. Generated source images remain in the workspace's `generated_images` directory. The shipping WebP files are encoded at quality 85 without changing the image dimensions; their combined size is about 3.4 MB. They are loaded on demand for the active Bishop rather than during initial scene startup.

The cutouts were inspected individually and composited over white to check alpha. A 150px contact sheet was inspected for pose continuity and phone-scale readability. This is technical inspection, not owner visual acceptance; staging, motion and impact must still be judged in the recorded game scenes.
