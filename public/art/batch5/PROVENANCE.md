# Batch 5 candidate artwork

The original character studies in `docs/reviews/artifacts/batch5-artwork/` were generated with OpenAI image generation from text descriptions. The owner approved those designs and choreography on October 29, 2026. The images in this directory are new generated cutouts derived from those original studies, encoded as WebP. No external illustration was supplied as a visual reference. The Librarian's long beard and two swords are character requirements, not traced source art. These cutouts and their motion remain subject to visual acceptance; generation alone is not a guarantee of uniqueness.

Each pose is an independently generated complete figure. The Librarian has a crossed guard, separate alternating sword positions, a crossed-blade contact pose and a recovery. Valhallarama has a vault and a left-facing broadside contact pose. Inspection of emulated videos and the owner's physical-phone decision are needed to judge transitions, timing and contact. The original generated outputs remain under `/workspace/generated_images/` in the authoring environment.

The Valhallarama contact cutout was replaced on November 4, 2026 with a new transparent image generated with OpenAI image generation using the existing `valhallarama-ready.webp` as the reference. It faces right with the sword extended toward a defender on that side. Its production motion and the resulting contact remain subject to owner visual acceptance.
