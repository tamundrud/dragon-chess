# Batch 4 candidate artwork

The original Stoick, Hookfang and Common or Garden studies were generated with OpenAI image generation for this project, without using book illustrations or screen-adaptation art. Flamehuffer's action pose was generated from the project's existing original Flamehuffer defender. Hookfang and Common or Garden ready poses were generated from their respective action studies. Stoick's later extended-axe tumble was generated from the approved Stoick pose and contact studies to make a visible contact possible; it preserves the original costume and character design. All game assets are WebP encodings of those complete cutouts.

The owner approved the Stoick and Flamehuffer artwork studies on 2026-10-29. This is artwork approval, not acceptance of the resulting motion. The source and earlier transparency/phone-scale inspection are retained in `docs/reviews/batch4-artwork-gate.md`. Hookfang and Common or Garden action cutouts were inspected against light and dark backgrounds at desktop and phone-emulated scale; their silhouettes lie close to the canvas edge. The extended-axe Stoick cutout has 25 pixels of minimum opaque margin. Character animation and actual-device performance still need owner and device review.
